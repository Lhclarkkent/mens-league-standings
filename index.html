Week 7: A 170-Point Explosion, a 123–123 Dead Heat, and One Team Still Perfect

Week 7 belonged to the extremes. Aaron Howell posted a week-high 170.5 in a 59-point rout of Kyle Avripas, Cody Brashear won by a whopping 69.5, and Nick's Play for Keeps fought James Bates to a 123.0-to-123.0 standstill before the tiebreak settled it for Bates — the week's wildest finish. Through it all, Sam Childers' Love Thy Nabers stayed perfect at 7-0, beating Jaden Robertson 138.5 to 122.0 and taking home the week's League Honor, Halfway Hero, with a league-best 1037.5 points scored so far this season.

At the halfway mark, every tab above tells part of the story: Global Standings shows the logjam tightening across all three leagues, Power Rankings shows who the numbers really trust, and Live Playoff Projections shows whose path is getting narrow. Week 8's marquee — James Bates' 6-1 McMahon's Headband vs. Calen McGinty's 6-1 Maye the Force Be with You — is the one to circle.
