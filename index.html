Week 4 Recap OCTOBER 6, 2026

Week 4: A 185.5-Point Eruption, the #12 Seed Shocks Apostles, and Three Teams Left Perfect

Week 4 belonged to the underdogs and the scoreboards. Josh Graven's Taylor Park Boys erupted for a season-high 185.5 in Patriarchs, while Jon Batchelor's winless Batch Squad — ranked #12 in Apostles — stunned Camfan1 138.5 to 113.0 for the week's biggest upset. In Faith Fighters, last week's marquee went to Calen's Maye the Force Be with You, who ended Gavin DeVore's perfect run 128.0 to 115.5. Through it all, just three teams across the three leagues are still unbeaten at 4-0, and two of them survived five-point scares to get there. Nick's Play for Keeps, meanwhile, stumbled to 1-3 in a 93.0-to-78.0 loss to Joes Team — the bounce-back starts now.

The season is taking shape, and the picture is clearest from the tabs above: check Global Standings to see where every team lands across all three leagues, Power Rankings to see who the numbers really believe in, and Live Playoff Projections to see which 4-0 starts are built to last. Week 5's marquee — Mike's Magnificent Team vs. the perfect Dart Vader — is the one to circle.
