Week 9 Recap NOVEMBER 10, 2026

Week 9: Perfection Falls, a 100-Point Rout, and a Half-Point Heartbreaker

Week 9 belonged to the fall of the perfect. Ben A ended Sam Childers' unbeaten run at 8-0 with a 152.0-point night, Will Tetley won by a full 100.5 points, and Drew Wilkins outlasted Mike Dodd 173.5 to 168.5 in a 342-point shootout — the week's two highest scores in one game. The week's League Honor, Defense, went to Jaden Robertson's Tatooine Tuskens, whose Bears D/ST pitched a 29.0-point masterclass. The one game that refused to follow the script was the squeaker: Josh Graven edged Chris Burke by just half a point.

With five weeks left, every tab above tells part of the story: Global Standings shows the logjam tightening across all three leagues, Power Rankings shows who the numbers really trust, and Live Playoff Projections shows whose path is getting narrow. Week 10's marquee — Chris Burke's 6-3 Bohemian Nixsody vs. Mike Dodd's 6-3 Mike's Magnificent Team — is a Patriarchs showdown with the division lead on the line.
