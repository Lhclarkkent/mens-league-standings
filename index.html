Week 11 Recap NOVEMBER 24, 2026

Week 11: A 108-Point Rout, a 164.5-Point Week High, and Two 3.5-Point Thrillers

Week 11 was a week of extremes. David Fletcher's Dart Vader posted the week's high with 164.5 points, Ben A flattened Parker Robbins by 108, and Sam Childers' Love Thy Nabers kept rolling at 10-1 with a 139.0 to 57.5 win. At the other end, two games were decided by just 3.5 points — Ezekiel Sanderson edging Kyle Avripas 151.5 to 148.0, and Wyatt Beasley nipping Chris Burke 126.0 to 122.5 — while Cohen Raby survived with the week's lowest winning score, 96.5, to take the Survivor honor.

With three weeks left, every tab above tells part of the story: Global Standings shows the logjam tightening across all three leagues, Power Rankings shows who the numbers really trust, and Live Playoff Projections shows whose path is getting narrow. Week 12's marquee — Kyle Avripas' 8-3 Gibbs me that Trophy! vs. Sam Childers' 10-1 Love Thy Nabers — is an Apostles showdown with the league's best record on the line.
