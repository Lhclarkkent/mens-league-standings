Week 5 Recap OCTOBER 13, 2026

Week 5: A 195-Point Eruption Ends Perfection, and Only One Unbeaten Team Remains

Week 5 was the week perfection got tested. Mike Dodd's Magnificent Team erupted for a league-high 195.0 to hand David Fletcher's Dart Vader its first loss, while Cody Brashear knocked off the other unbeaten, James Bates' McMahon's Headband, 119.0 to 87.0. That leaves exactly one perfect team standing: Sam Childers' Love Thy Nabers, now 5-0 after a 187.0-point rout of Jonah Atkins. Elsewhere, Aaron Howell delivered the week's biggest blowout — 158.5 to 76.5 over Parker Robbins — and Josh Graven survived the week's tightest finish, edging Wyatt Beasley by five. Nick's Play for Keeps fell to 1-4 in a low-scoring 81.0-to-68.5 loss to Morgan Jones' Dart Knight — the bounce-back starts in Week 6.

The race is tightening, and the picture is clearest from the tabs above: check Global Standings to see where every team lands across all three leagues, Power Rankings to see who the numbers really believe in, and Live Playoff Projections to see whether the last unbeaten run is built to last. Week 6's marquee — the perfect Love Thy Nabers vs. Troy Graham's Promised Loveland — is the one to circle.
