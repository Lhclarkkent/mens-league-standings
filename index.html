Cross-Division Breakdown ROUND 1-3 ANALYSIS

Draft Night Analysis & Team Grades

In a massive 36-team super league split across three 12-team rooms (Faith Fighters, Apostles, and Patriarchs), draft strategy shifted heavily toward securing elite quarterback floors and bell-cow running backs early before talent evaporated.

Faith Fighters Division Recap

Round 1 Highlights: Drake's Daring Team kicked off the draft by grabbing Josh Allen (BUF, QB), while Cohen's Team went with Jayden Daniels (WSH, QB) and Dart Knights secured Bijan Robinson (ATL, RB). Later in the first, Jesus Wept grabbed Jahmyr Gibbs and Turned Up Tunics landed Christian McCaffrey.

Grade & Commentary: Grade: A. Managers in Faith Fighters prioritized elite dual-threat quarterbacks early, locking down positional advantages before positional runs dried up.

Apostles Division Recap

Round 1 Highlights: Kyle's Top-Notch Team kicked off the room with Jahmyr Gibbs (DET, RB), followed by The Promised Loveland taking Josh Allen (BUF, QB) and Tatooine Tuskens selecting Lamar Jackson (BAL, QB). Heavy hitters like Bijan Robinson and Ja'Marr Chase flew off the board immediately.

Grade & Commentary: Grade: A-. Excellent balance of elite rushing floors and high-upside wide receiver anchors in rounds 2 and 3.

Patriarchs Division Recap

Round 1 Highlights: Braxton's Beastly Team started strong with Jahmyr Gibbs (DET, RB), Armor Bearers secured Josh Allen (BUF, QB), and Blitzburg snagged Bijan Robinson (ATL, RB). Elite quarterback runs defined the top of this room as well.

Grade & Commentary: Grade: A. Highly aggressive early-round drafting with phenomenal value snags in rounds 2 and 3.
